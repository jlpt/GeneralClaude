Neurogenesis Automaton

A DNA string is read into growth rules, and a grid automaton runs those rules from a single cell. Neurons divide, release signals and grow axons; an axon that reaches a neuron it targets becomes a synapse.

Growing Step 0 · 1 neuron · 0 synapses

Hover or tap a neuron to trace its synapses.

Each step updates the 48 × 48 grid locally. Signals diffuse to neighbouring cells, neurons divide into nearby empty cells, and each axon tip moves one cell, following or avoiding the signal of the types it targets. Lines end in a dot for an excitatory synapse and a bar for an inhibitory one.

Genome

5′3′

A gene starts at ATG and ends at a stop codon (TAA, TAG, TGA). Each codon of three bases is read as a number from 0 to 63, and a gene's first 16 codons set the rules for one neuron type. Gene 1 makes the zygote, the cell that every neuron grows from. Click a gene in the bar to find it in the sequence.
