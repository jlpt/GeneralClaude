Neurogenesis Forager

One cell and a random growth rule grow a jointed body with a nervous system inside it. To stay alive it has to find fruit: eyes see fruit ahead, every nerve cell can feel hunger, and moving costs energy. Evolution keeps the growth rules whose creatures eat the most, and the genome bench below lets you change the numbers yourself and grow the result.

All the numbers

Growth

Over 40 steps, every cell reads its neighbours and updates its 18 numbers with the same small network, starting from a single cell on a 30 × 42 grid. Every 3 × 3 patch with enough living cells becomes a joint, joined by a bone to the patch it grew from.

A joint becomes bone, muscle or eye, whichever of those numbers its cells hold most of. Muscle joints are hinges; eyes are rigid and see.

Senses and nerves

Any small cell may grow a nerve cell. They form a spiking recurrent network: each has a charge that leaks, fires at a threshold, and sends weighted spikes only to nerve cells touching it. A muscle joint bends when the nerve cells in its patch fire.

Nerve cells can feel four things: touch (in joints on the ground), a slow pacemaker rhythm, sight (in an eye, brighter the nearer the fruit ahead of it) and hunger. Each eye looks away from the body's middle.

Food and evolution

Four fruit lie beyond the creature's ends, farther away than it is tall, so falling over doesn't reach them. A joint touching one eats it, and a new one appears farther off. Energy drains with time, faster for big bodies and busy muscles; a full stomach lasts about 12 seconds.

Each generation adds small random changes to the genome, tests every copy in parallel workers, and moves toward the changes that scored best. When a copy beats the best so far on the re-test, the search jumps to it.

Take a genome to the Python version

Press Copy as text for Python, save it as best.txt in the project folder, and turn it into a file render.py can draw:

python -c "import base64, numpy as np; np.save('best.npy', np.frombuffer(base64.b64decode(open('best.txt').read()), np.float32))"
python render.py best.npy --out best.gif --world 1000
